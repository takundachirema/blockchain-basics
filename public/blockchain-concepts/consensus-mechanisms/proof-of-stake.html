Proof of Stake (PoS)

How PoS Works

In Proof of Stake, validators lock funds as stake. The protocol selects validators to propose and attest to blocks, often using stake-weighted selection plus randomness.

  • Validators must keep stake locked as collateral.
  • Blocks are finalized through validator voting/attestations.
  • Energy use is far lower than PoW mining.

Block Rewards in PoS

Honest validators typically earn:

  • Protocol rewards: Issuance for proposing and attesting correctly.
  • Transaction fees/tips: Depending on protocol design.

The system is designed so consistent uptime and honest votes produce steady returns.

Cost of Being Dishonest in PoS

  • Slashing risk: Malicious or conflicting behavior can burn a portion of the validator’s stake.
  • Penalty risk: Offline or poor behavior can reduce rewards.
  • Large capital at risk: Coordinated attacks need large stake exposure that can be penalized.
  • Economic self-harm: Attacks can reduce trust and token value, hurting the attacker’s own staked position.
Security intuition: PoS secures the chain by requiring attackers to place valuable collateral at risk and by directly punishing dishonest behavior.
